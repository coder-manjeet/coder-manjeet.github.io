01 / About

Engineering with product thinking.

I care about the whole lifecycle: understanding the problem, shaping the architecture, building the experience and making the result maintainable.

My core experience is in PHP and Laravel, building business applications, APIs and multi-tenant SaaS products. I work across the stack with React, Vue, Inertia, TypeScript and modern component systems, and I’m comfortable integrating third-party services and designing data-heavy workflows.
