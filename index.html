01 / MISSION CONTROL

I build software platforms that can think, connect and act.

My focus is the convergence of robust business software and agentic AI — not chatbots bolted onto dashboards, but useful agents grounded in real application capabilities.

My foundation is full-stack product engineering with PHP and Laravel, backed by React, Vue, Inertia and TypeScript. I build systems with real business rules: users, permissions, workflows, records, approvals, reporting, integrations and tenant boundaries.

My next chapter is making those systems agent-ready. I design MCP servers that expose safe, purpose-built tools from an application, then connect agents to those tools so they can assist with operational tasks while respecting permissions, validation and human approval.

That means an ERP agent that can prepare a report, an HRM assistant that can guide an onboarding workflow, or a trial-management agent that can surface missing visit data — with the application remaining the source of truth.
